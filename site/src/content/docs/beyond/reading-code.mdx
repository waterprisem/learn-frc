<WhyItMatters>
On a real team you will join a robot project that is already thousands of lines long.
You cannot read it all, and you do not need to. A fixed method gets you to the one piece you are asked to change in minutes.
</WhyItMatters>

## The method

1. **Open `RobotContainer` first.** It is the table of contents: it creates the subsystems, binds buttons, and sets default commands.
2. **List the subsystems** it creates. That is the robot's list of mechanisms.
3. **Pick one button binding.**
4. **Follow it to the command.** Use Go to Definition.
5. **Follow the command into the subsystem method** it calls.
6. **Follow that to `Constants`** to see the numbers behind it.
7. **Read telemetry names** (the topics a subsystem publishes). They show which signals the team thinks matter.

Use **Go to Definition** and **Find All References** (lesson 0.6) to hop. Searching for `TODO` finds unfinished work. Do not read every file.

## A worked example: Rebuilt2026

The team's 2026 robot code is public at `github.com/FRC1360/Rebuilt2026`. Start in `RobotContainer.java`.
It creates `m_intakeSubsystem`, `m_indexSubsystem`, `m_flywheelSubsystem`, `m_HoodSubsystem`, and `m_TurretSubsystem`, plus the swerve drivetrain.
Near the top of `configureBindings()`, the driver controller's `povRight()` is stored as `backdriveShooterInput`. Trace that button.

**Step 4: constants and telemetry.** In `Constants.java`, `FlywheelConstants` holds the CAN IDs (`VORTEX_LEADER_CAN_ID`, `VORTEX_FOLLOWER_CAN_ID`), current limits, and the tolerance.
The subsystem publishes its signals under `Subsystems/` plus its name, such as `Flywheel Leader Current`. Those names tell you what the team watches.

The whole chain: button, trigger, command, subsystem method, constants. Five hops, and you never opened the swerve or vision files.

<GoDeeper title="why the binding is more than one command">
Real bindings combine triggers with `and`, `or`, and `negate`. Here, the shooting inputs are blocked while any backdrive button is held.
Read the trigger definitions near the top of `configureBindings()` before the `whileTrue` lines.
</GoDeeper>
