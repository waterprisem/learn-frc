A competition robot can move fast enough and hard enough to hurt someone, and code you just deployed has never been tested.

## Disable and emergency stop

Your robot only drives its motors while **enabled**. **Disabled** is the safe state.

In the Driver Station:

- **Enter** disables the robot.
- **Space bar** is the **emergency stop** (E-stop). It stops the robot immediately.

An E-stopped robot must be restarted before it can be enabled again. Keep a hand near the keyboard whenever the robot is enabled.

The first thing to reach for in an electrical emergency is the red main breaker, which cuts all power.

## Before you enable

<ol>
<li>Everyone is clear of the mechanisms, wheels, and any moving arm or elevator.</li>
<li>Safety glasses are on for everyone near the robot.</li>
<li>The robot is on blocks if the wheels could move it.</li>
<li>The person at the Driver Station knows the code that was just deployed.</li>
<li>Call out "ENABLING!" loudly.</li>
<li>Wait for people nearby to answer "clear" before you enable.</li>
</ol>

## Hands, power, and batteries

- Turn the **main breaker off** before touching wiring or reaching into a mechanism.
- Stay out of reach of anything that can move. Keep hands, hair, and loose clothing away from chains, gears, and wheels.
- **Brake mode does not make a mechanism safe.** It resists motion only while the motor is powered and the controller is working. An arm or elevator can still fall when power is cut, so never stand or reach under a raised mechanism.
- Carry a battery by its **case**, never by its cables.
- Do not use a damaged or leaking battery. Tell a mentor.

## Checkpoint

<Checkpoint lesson="1.14">

<details>
<summary>The robot starts driving toward a teammate. What do you press, and what must happen before you can enable it again?</summary>
Press the Space bar to E-stop (Enter only disables). The robot must then be restarted before it can be enabled again.
</details>

<details>
<summary>A teammate wants to reach into the intake to free a jammed game piece. What do you do first?</summary>
Disable the robot, and turn the main breaker off before anyone puts a hand in. A disabled robot with a live breaker can still be surprising when it re-enables.
</details>

</Checkpoint>
